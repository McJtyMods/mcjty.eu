# Gravity Hex

## Introduction

Gravity Hex is a puzzle game where you guide a series of balls from a starting point to an exit.
You have to prepare first. Shape the terrain, destroy hazards, ... and then when released the balls will roll and bounce around
until they reach the exit or get stuck. There are ads in this game but only on demand. i.e. you will not see any ads
unless you want to watch one to get an item receive a hint. Enjoy!

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 2026

Thank you for playing **Gravity Hex**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the game and how that information is used.

### Information Collected

Gravity Hex does **not** require a user account and does **not** directly collect or store personally identifiable information.

Game progress, settings, and highscores are stored locally on your device.

### Rewarded Advertisements

Gravity Hex uses **Google AdMob** to display **optional rewarded advertisements**.

Advertisements are **never shown automatically**. An advertisement is only displayed when you explicitly choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward, such as receiving a bonus item or continuing after losing.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as:

- Device identifiers
- IP address
- Advertising identifiers
- Diagnostic information
- Information required for fraud prevention and ad delivery

The collection and processing of this information is performed by Google in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For more information, please refer to:

- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

### Data Storage

Gravity Hex stores the following information locally on your device:

- Game settings
- Game progress
- Owned bonus items

This information is not transmitted to the developer.

### Third-Party Services

Gravity Hex currently uses the following third-party service:

- Google AdMob (Google LLC)

### Children's Privacy

Gravity Hex is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable privacy laws.
